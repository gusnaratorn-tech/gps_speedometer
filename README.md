# GPS Speedometer & Odometer App (Android) 🚗💨

A high-performance Cyberpunk-themed **GPS Speedometer, Odometer, Drag Racing Timer & HUD App** built with Flutter & Kotlin for Android.

## ✨ Features

- 🏎️ **Multiple Dashboards**:
  - **Gauge View**: Analog dial with glowing neon needle and active speed progress arc.
  - **Digital View**: Big high-contrast digital digits.
  - **HUD Mode (Head-Up Display)**: Windshield mirror-flip projection with Fullscreen black background.
- ⏱️ **Drag Racing & Performance Mode**:
  - Christmas Tree launch countdown lights (Pre-Stage, Staged, Green GO).
  - Auto-start detection when launching from 0 km/h.
  - Split times: **0-60 km/h**, **0-100 km/h**, and **1/4 Mile (402m)** with trap speed.
  - Real-time **Longitudinal G-Force Meter** and Peak Acceleration recording.
- 📊 **Real-Time Telemetry & Sensors**:
  - Real-time sparkline speed curve graph.
  - Digital compass heading (0-360° with cardinal direction, e.g. `245° SW`).
  - Altitude above sea level (meters) and GPS signal accuracy indicator.
- 🔔 **Multi-Tone Audio & Vibration Warning**:
  - Adaptive pulsing warning when exceeding speed limit (chimes accelerate as margin increases).
  - 4 Selectable audio tone styles (Cyberpunk, Supercar, Sci-Fi Arcade, Sport Bell).
  - Haptic vibration feedback.
- 📷 **Speed Camera & Radar Alerts**:
  - Proximity warning banner and radar audio chime when approaching speed cameras within 800m.
  - In-app custom speed camera marking tool.
- ⛽ **Fuel Economy & Cost Calculator**:
  - Real-time fuel consumption (Liters) and estimated trip cost (฿).
- 📜 **Trip History Logbook & Telemetry Share Card**:
  - Auto-save trip stats on pause/reset.
  - Export hi-res Telemetry Graphic Summary Cards (PNG).
  - Export standard GPX / CSV routes.
- 🪟 **Floating Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**:
  - Shrink speedometer into a floating overlay window above Google Maps or Waze.
- 🔒 **Native Screen Wakelock**:
  - Automatically keeps screen awake while tracking or in HUD mode.
